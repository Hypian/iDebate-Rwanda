Pedagogical Framework

Curriculum Architecture: Two Phases of Professional Transformation

A sustained dual-tiered methodology delivering intensive classroom debate mastery followed by prolonged, year-round field coaching.

PHASE I // RESIDENTIAL LAB menu_book

Summer Intensive Workshop

Teachers gather for a week during the summer for intensive training in the process of debate. They're introduced to the mechanisms of debate and different ways to improve their students' argumentation skills, speech construction, and critical enquiry.

IMMERSION BENCHMARKS

  • check_circle 40+ hours of pedagogical immersion & parliamentary dialectics
  • check_circle Standardized rubrics for evaluating complex parliamentary motions
  • check_circle Classroom debate staging techniques, rebuttal drills, and adjudicator guidelines
PHASE II // FIELD SUPERVISION school

Peer to Peer Sessions & Mentorship

After the summer Debate Institute, teachers undergo monthly one-on-one coaching sessions with iDebate head trainers, with refresher courses to share lessons learned — usually a full-day session of collaborative reflection and curriculum alignment.

COLLABORATIVE BENCHMARKS

  • check_circle Monthly diagnostic coaching alongside certified senior adjudicators
  • check_circle Inter-school peer lesson observation and pedagogical critique
  • check_circle Continuous professional development portfolio & accredited renewal badges

Coach of the Year Award

Every year, iDebate Rwanda recognizes an outstanding debate coach whose dedication and impact has profoundly transformed their school's debate culture. The Coach of the Year award celebrates the individuals who bring the classroom to life — nurturing Rwanda's next generation of critical thinkers and civic leaders.

Presented annually at the Teachers' Debate Institute closing ceremony.
