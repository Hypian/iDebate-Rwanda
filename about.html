ESTABLISHED 2012 // KIGALI, RWANDA

Cultivating the courage to speak, the patience to listen, and the wisdom to reconcile.

Founded in 2012 in the aftermath of Rwanda's transformative journey of reconciliation, iDebate Rwanda is an educational civic initiative dedicated to shaping a generation of young Africans who resolve disagreement through rigorous reason rather than hostility.

We believe that genuine peace is not simply the polite absence of tension, but the presence of structured, empathetic civil discourse. Across secondary schools in every province, we equip young minds with the dialectical tools to question dogmatism, analyze public policy, and advocate with ethical conviction.

THE HISTORICAL IMPERATIVE

Why Debate is an Urgent Tool of Peace in Rwanda

In 1994, words were weaponized to orchestrate the Genocide against the Tutsi. Radio broadcasts and political rhetoric incited neighbors against neighbors, demonstrating with horrific clarity the catastrophic power of unexamined propaganda when received by an unquestioning public.

In the decades of nation-building that followed, Rwandan youth were often taught that maintaining peace meant avoiding contentious conversation altogether. Silence was frequently mistaken for social harmony. But silence leaves young minds vulnerable to dogma and demagoguery.

iDebate Rwanda was established to break that silence constructively. By teaching young scholars how to disagree on complex socio-economic and ethical issues using logic, research, and respect, we create an unshakeable foundation for generational peace.

menu_book PEDAGOGICAL CORE

The Dialectical Method

Unlike informal arguments where emotions govern assertions, competitive academic debate enforces strict evidence standards, structured speaking times, and mandatory perspective switching—ensuring debaters examine issues from all angles before reaching conclusions.

GUIDING PHILOSOPHY

Our Four Foundational Principles

Every training module, tournament motion, and classroom workshop is anchored in four core intellectual standards.

01
UKURI // TRUTH

Rigor in Inquiry

No assertion without evidence. We teach scholars to cross-examine primary sources, interrogate statistics, dismantle logical fallacies, and respect verifiable facts.

Evidence-Based Advocacy
02
KUMVA UNDI // EMPATHY

Perspective-Taking

Debaters do not choose their side in competition. To win, they must articulate the strongest possible argument for opposing worldviews—the ultimate antidote to dogmatism.

Cognitive Empathy
03
URUHARE // ACTION

Civic Agency

Eloquence without ethical purpose is hollow. We mentor students to translate debate motions into grassroots community initiatives, environmental action, and policy dialogues.

Translating Words to Action
04
UBUSABANE // INCLUSION

Radical Inclusivity

Democratic speech must never be the monopoly of an elite few. From rural public schools in Nyamagabe to urban academies in Kigali, our platform belongs to all Rwandan youth.

Universal Access to Discourse
