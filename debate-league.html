Debate League

"A competitive person always wins."

Our monthly competitions serve as a venue for our students to practice all they learn during respective training sessions as they become better speakers, not only theoretically but practically. Our students meet on a monthly basis to debate Public Policy Issues across 3 provinces (Kigali, Eastern Province and Southern Province of Rwanda) with over 115 schools and more than 5000 students.

Strategic Educational Mandate

Objectives of the League

Transforming Rwandan secondary schools into incubation grounds for open inquiries, democratic habits, and public evidence evaluation.

Pillar 01

Create a Network of Debate Clubs in Rwanda

Research shows that participation in debate increases a student's ability to think critically and creatively about community problems. Our vision is that every school in Rwanda has a debate club.

CIVIC INSTITUTIONAL CAPACITY account_tree
Pillar 02

Improve Critical Thinking and Public Speaking Skills

Critical thinking is a habit of the mind characterized by comprehensive exploration of issues, ideas, artifacts and events before forming a conclusion — essential for the 21st century.

RHETORICAL & ANALYTIC RIGOR psychology
Pillar 03

Create a Platform for Young People to Share Ideas

Young people represent both the future of the country and its current force. Debate gives them a platform to discuss the issues affecting their communities and shape solutions.

Structured Progression Framework

League Divisions

A dual-tiered pedagogy engineered to accompany students from their very first time holding a microphone to rigorous inter-scholastic national tournaments.

Division 01 // Foundation MIN 12 MONTHS

Beginners League

The entry point for schools/students new to the program, focused on overcoming fear of public speaking and building confidence through debate fundamentals. Students stay in this division at least 12 months before graduating to the Advanced League.

Student Led Competition

Small competitions with a maximum of 4 participating schools, run in an elimination format completed in a single 3-hour afternoon.

Register for Beginners League
Division 02 // Mastery REGIONAL & NATIONAL

Advanced League

Teams in the Advanced League are graduates of the Beginners League, competing at a higher level of argumentation, rapid cross-examination, and empirical public policy research.

Championship Format

Regional round-robins leading to national quarter, semi, and grand finals broadcast to nationwide audiences.
